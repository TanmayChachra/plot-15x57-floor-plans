# 20 floor plans for a 15.4 ft x 57.6 ft plot

Ground floor commercial, first floor residential, terrace at second floor level. Plot 887 sq ft (82.4 sq m, 98.6 sq yd).

Gallery: see the GitHub Pages link in the repository description. Full sheets are in `docs/plans/`.

## What was asked and how it is met
- Front as big as possible: the shop shutter is 9'-0" wide, the stair door 3'-0", the rest of the 15'-4.8" front is wall piers.
- Stair starts at the front: its own street door, entry lobby, then two straight flights along the left party wall (ground to first, first to terrace). Own entrance means the shop and the home never share a door.
- No parking built.
- Indian concepts: central open court (angan, Brahmasthan) on the first floor, passage (verandah) beside it, jaali window to the court, terrace tank in the SW/W/S, parapet and rainwater outlets, mumty over the stair.
- Vastu: every room centroid is placed on the 3 x 3 grid of the plot (thirds) for the chosen facing and scored ideal, acceptable, compromise or avoid. A plan is published only when nothing is in an "avoid" zone (toilet in NE/E/SW/centre, kitchen in NE/SW/centre, stair in NE/N/E/centre, master in NE/SE/centre, shop entrance outside the good half, tank in NE/N/E).
- Not every room can sit in an ideal zone on a 15 ft frontage. Scores are printed on each sheet. East and North facing score best.

## Assumptions (not given by you, please confirm)
- Road side unknown, so plans exist for East, North, South and West facing.
- Road in front, party walls both sides (no side setback), 5 ft rear yard, building footprint 15.4 x 52.6 ft.
- 9 in outer walls, 4.5 in partitions, 5 in RCC slabs, floor to floor 10'-6".
- Floor level 6 in above the road. A higher plinth needs a front setback for steps or a ramp.
- Setbacks, FAR, plinth, light-court size and balcony rules differ by city. Confirm with the local building bylaws. A licensed architect and structural engineer must finalise drawings.

## Stair data (checked)
18 risers x 7 in (178 mm, limit 190), tread 10 in (254 mm, limit 250), 3'-6" clear width, 35 degree pitch, headroom 7.2 ft (2.2 m) or more everywhere (slab opening starts 8.7 ft from the front wall inner face), straight flights, 3'-6" landing at first floor level, mumty over the terrace exit.

## Checks run by script on every plan
No overlapping rooms, walls at least 4.5 in, every room reachable by a door from the street or stair, master and living at least 7.9 ft wide, second bedroom 7.0 ft, kitchen at least 48 sq ft and 5.9 ft wide, toilets at least 24 sq ft and 4 ft wide, openings at least 1/6 of floor area (bedrooms, living), 1/8 (hall, kitchen, cabin), 1/10 (toilets, shop). Thresholds are common bylaw rules of thumb, not a legal certificate.

Known limits: the living-dining (2 BHK plans) and dining/foyer (1 BHK plans) get their light only from the 6 x 8 ft open court, which some local bylaws may not accept for habitable rooms. The 1 BHK plans have a WC but only one bedroom. The set has 18 distinct layouts and 2 repeats that differ only in the terrace.
